## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an elevated travel and hospitality experience, balancing the timeless prestige of luxury editorial publishing with the disciplined utility of modern SaaS interface design.

### Aesthetic Foundation
- **Modern Editorial Luxury**: High-contrast typography pairings elevate standard booking flows into curated journeys. The interplay between traditional serif display typography and crisp grotesque body copy projects authority, heritage, and white-glove curation.
- **Architectural Utility**: Borrowing from Untitled UI's structured design ethos, interface containers remain disciplined, modular, and unencumbered by unnecessary visual noise. Spatial density is controlled via precise paddings, hairline dividers, and standardized icon framing.
- **Tone & Mood**: Trustworthy, bespoke, serene, and confident. The visual tone evokes private aviation, heritage estate retreats, and bespoke destination concierge services without feeling inaccessible or sluggish.

## Colors

The color architecture enforces a high-contrast monochrome foundation, elevated by organic botanicals, heritage metallics, and deliberate transactional signals.

### Color Tokens & Roles
- **Text & Structure (Neutrals)**:
  - `text-primary`: `#171717` — Core display headings, high-importance data labels, and primary buttons.
  - `text-secondary`: `#475467` — Subtitles, supporting copy, and descriptive tour itineraries.
  - `text-tertiary`: `#667085` — Meta information, flight tags, and auxiliary captions.
  - `surface-canvas`: `#FAFAFA` — Page-level base canvas.
  - `surface-panel`: `#FFFFFF` — Primary elevated cards, modal sheets, and floating bars.
  - `surface-muted`: `#F8FAFC` — Subtle container fills, table alternating stripes, and inactive states.
  - `border-subtle`: `#EAECF0` — Default component boundaries and light separators.
  - `border-bold`: `#E4E7EC` — Active input outlines, table header dividing lines, and card containers.

- **Primary Accent (Tea-Green)**:
  - `primary`: `#0F6B4A` — Primary interactive brand color, key booking CTAs, vetted rating badges, and active tab highlights.
  - `primary-hover`: `#065F46` — Hover and focused states for primary buttons.
  - `primary-surface`: `#ECFDF5` — Tinted badge backdrops, highlighted perks, and success toasts.

- **Secondary Accent (Refined Gold Laurel)**:
  - `secondary`: `#B8924A` — Curated accolades, star ratings, VIP/connoisseur tier badges, and verified guide markers.
  - `secondary-surface`: `#FDFBF7` — Tinted VIP callout backgrounds.

- **Tertiary Accent (Direct Messaging)**:
  - `tertiary`: `#25D366` — Dedicated WhatsApp concierge action triggers, instant departure chat availability, and live support states.
  - `tertiary-hover`: `#1EBE5D` — Active interactive state for direct messaging channels.

## Typography

The typographical hierarchy is built on an editorial-functional split:
- **Headings (`Noto Serif` / `DM Serif Display`)**: Used exclusively for titles, section headers, narrative pull-quotes, and package names. Always set with tight line-heights and slight negative tracking at large scales to avoid loose letterforms.
- **Body, Labels & Financials (`Manrope`)**: Provides exceptional clarity for complex metadata, pricing tables, booking schedules, and micro-copy. 
- **Tabular Figures**: All numeric data, including prices, dates, durations, and seats remaining, must use tabular font feature settings (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to maintain alignment across table grids and pricing cards.

## Layout & Spacing

A structured 12-column grid system anchors the presentation of tours, filters, and booking modules.

### Grid & Canvas Structure
- **Desktop (>= 1280px)**: 12 columns, max-width `1280px`, `1.5rem` gutters, `2rem` outer page margins.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: 8 columns, fluid margins with a minimum of `1.5rem`, `1.25rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (<= 767px)**: 4 columns, `1rem` outer gutters and margins. Sticky footers house booking CTAs to maximize vertical viewport space.

### Structural Rhythm
- Element padding inside cards follows strict powers of 8: `1.5rem` (`space-lg`) for primary tour cards and `2rem` (`space-xl`) for modal dialogs and summary checkout modules.
- Layout sections employ generous breathing room (`space-3xl` / `4.5rem`) between thematic groupings to maintain the curated editorial tempo.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy shadows, relying on layered neutral borders combined with Untitled UI-style diffused ambient shadows.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Flat / Inset)**: No shadow. Border: `1px solid #EAECF0`. Applied to standard list items, input fields, and recessed containers (`#F8FAFC`).
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Blocks)**:
  - `box-shadow: 0px 1px 3px rgba(16, 24, 40, 0.08), 0px 1px 2px rgba(16, 24, 40, 0.04);`
  - Border: `1px solid #EAECF0`.
  - Used for standard tour cards, review blocks, and pricing containers.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Interactive Focus)**:
  - `box-shadow: 0px 4px 8px -2px rgba(16, 24, 40, 0.08), 0px 2px 4px -2px rgba(16, 24, 40, 0.04);`
  - Border: `1px solid #D0D5DD`.
  - Applied when hovering over tour cards, itinerary steps, and selectable pricing tiers.
- **Level 3 (Flyouts, Menus & Dropdowns)**:
  - `box-shadow: 0px 12px 16px -4px rgba(16, 24, 40, 0.06), 0px 4px 6px -2px rgba(16, 24, 40, 0.03);`
  - Border: `1px solid #EAECF0`.
  - Applied to date-pickers, guest-selectors, and filter popovers.
- **Level 4 (Modals & Overlays)**:
  - `box-shadow: 0px 20px 24px -4px rgba(16, 24, 40, 0.08), 0px 8px 8px -4px rgba(16, 24, 40, 0.03);`
  - Used for checkout modals, full itinerary galleries, and mobile bottom drawers.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates geometric precision through balanced curved forms:
- **Base Components (Inputs, Small Badges, Buttons)**: `8px` (`0.5rem`) for compact interactions and structured control fields.
- **Containers & Cards (`rounded-xl` / `rounded-2xl`)**:
  - `rounded-xl` (`16px` / `1rem`): Search filter docks, tabular panels, and thumbnail media frames.
  - `rounded-2xl` (`24px` / `1.5rem`): Main tour packaging cards, hero image wrappers, and testimonial blocks.
- **Icon Enclosures & Status Pills**: Full pill (`9999px`) roundedness for rating counters, tag labels, and conversational action buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Tea-Green)**: Background `#0F6B4A`, foreground `#FFFFFF`, height `44px` (desktop) or `40px` (mobile), padding `0 18px`, font `Manrope` 600. Hover `#065F46`. Focus ring `4px #ECFDF5`.
- **Secondary (Monochrome Outline)**: Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #D0D5DD`, text `#344054`. Hover background `#F9FAFB`.
- **WhatsApp Concierge**: Background `#25D366`, foreground `#FFFFFF`, icon prefixed, height `44px`, pill-rounded (`9999px`). Hover `#1EBE5D`. Focus ring `4px rgba(37, 211, 102, 0.2)`.

### Featured Icon Badges
- Untitled UI pattern: `48px` or `40px` circular or squircle containers (`radius: 10px`).
- Background: `#ECFDF5` for tea-green accents, `#FDFBF7` for gold laurel accolades, and `#F2F4F7` for structural utilities.
- Inner icon: `24px` centered vector, color `#0F6B4A` or `#B8924A`. Border: `1px solid` matching 20% alpha tint.

### Tour & Package Cards (`rounded-2xl`)
- White surface, `1px solid #EAECF0`, Level 1 elevation, smooth transition `200ms ease`.
- Top: Image slot with `16px` inner inset or flush-bleed with `rounded-t-2xl`. Image contains floating top-left badge (Tea-Green or Gold Laurel) and top-right wishlist action (`#FFFFFF` pill circle).
- Content Area: `20px` padding. Headline in `Noto Serif` (`20px`), metadata bar (duration, pax, location) in `Manrope` (`13px`, `#667085`), footer containing bold `price-card` tabular value alongside a compact primary button.

### Form Inputs & Search Filters
- Input fields: Height `44px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #D0D5DD`, font `Manrope` `14px`, text `#171717`, placeholder `#667085`.
- Active focus: Border `#0F6B4A`, ring `3px rgba(15, 107, 74, 0.12)`.
- Global Search Dock: Horizontal pill or `rounded-xl` bar housing segmented modules: Destination, Date Range, Guests, and CTA. Separated by hairline vertical dividers (`#EAECF0`).

### Chips & Badges
- **Editorial Category Chips**: Neutral background `#F8FAFC`, border `1px solid #EAECF0`, font `Manrope` `12px` font-weight 600, color `#344054`.
- **Gold Laurel Tag**: Background `#FDFBF7`, border `1px solid rgba(184, 146, 74, 0.3)`, text `#B8924A`, accompanied by a star or laurel leaf icon.

### Tabular Layouts (Itineraries & Rate Sheets)
- Crisp spreadsheet-inspired clarity. Table headers: uppercase `Manrope` `12px`, tracking `0.05em`, text `#475467`, background `#FAFAFA`, bottom border `1px solid #EAECF0`.
- Cells: `16px` vertical padding, font `Manrope` `14px`, numeric alignment right-aligned with monospace numeric features.
